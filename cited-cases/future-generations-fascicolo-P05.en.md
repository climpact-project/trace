## 0. What it is for and how to read it

You are given a passage from ANOTHER case. You must say whether it takes up a proposition from Future Generations (RIPRESA), whether it asserts something similar based on another source (ALTRA_FONTE), or whether it does not assert it (NON_AFFERMA). Links in the chain of argument, in the order in which the Supreme Court traverses them: (a) justiciability by conexidad: harm to the environment, a collective right, harms fundamental individual rights, and the action proceeds despite the acción popular; minors act in their own name; (b) foundation: fundamental rights are determined by the ecosystem; the ecocentric-anthropic criterion overcomes anthropocentrism; future generations are holders of environmental rights that impose on present generations a duty of non-action; (c) fact and causation: deforestation of the Amazon (+44% between 2015 and 2016) releases CO2 and produces the greenhouse effect, imminent and serious harm to present and future generations; verified with the principles of precaution, intergenerational equity (the transgression is obvious, because the minor claimants are the future generation) and solidarity (duty and co-responsibility of the State to stop the causes); (d) breach: the Paris commitment to zero deforestation by 2020 and the omissions of the environmental authorities, the parks, the departments, the municipalities; (e) remedy: the Colombian Amazon is a subject of rights, as the Corte Constitucional had done for the Atrato River, and the authorities must formulate plans with the active participation of the claimants, including an intergenerational pact.

Four things to always keep in mind.

1. The authorship of the ideas is declared by the Court itself. Nature as a subject of rights comes from T-622/2016 (Atrato), cited at length at pp. 39-41 and referred to in the operative part itself («tal como la Corte Constitucional declaró al río Atrato», p. 45). The conexidad environment-rights, the heritage to be delivered to future generations, the «Constitución Ecológica», the triple dimensión of the healthy environment come from the Corte Constitucional (T-411/1992, C-431/2000, C-449/2015, T-362/2014, SU-1116/2001), cited at length at pp. 11-13 and 27-30. The «derechos-deberes» come from Peces-Barba (p. 18), the solidaridad diacrónica from Rodríguez Palop (p. 19). What is proper to Future Generations is the APPLICATION of those ideas to climate and deforestation, with four moves that none of the sources makes: fundamental rights «determinados por el entorno y el ecosistema» with the «seres sintientes» alongside the «sujetos de derecho» (p. 13); the environmental rights of future generations as «relación jurídica obligatoria» and «prestación de “no-hacer”» that limits the freedom of present generations (p. 21); the criterion «ecocéntrico - antrópico» that «sitúa al ser humano a la par del entorno ecosistémico» (pp. 20-21); the extension of legal personhood from a river to an entire biome as a remedy for a climate claim (p. 45). Whoever takes up nature as a subject of rights by citing the Atrato takes up the Atrato, not Future Generations.

2. Future generations here are HOLDERS of rights, and the minor claimants are that generation. It is the exact opposite of Neubauer, where future generations «do not yet carry any fundamental rights in the present» and the duty towards them «has a solely objective dimension» (see 4.7). A text that speaks of future generations as bearers of subjective rights that limit present generations is on the track of Future Generations; a text that speaks of intertemporal freedom of the living or of an objective duty of the State is on that of Neubauer; a text that does not decide on future generations because the present generation suffices (Hof Urgenda § 37) is on that of Urgenda.

3. The name of the case is a common phrase. «Future generations», «generaciones futuras», «Amazonía», «Minambiente» alone indicate nothing: they appear in the preamble of the UNFCCC, in Principle 3 of Rio, in every recent constitution. The name counts as a clue only in the compound forms of the case file (Future Generations v. Ministry of the Environment, Demanda Generaciones Futuras v. Minambiente, STC4360-2018, 11001-22-03-000-2018-00319-01, Lozano Barragán, Rodríguez Peña, Tolosa Villabona, «Colombian Supreme Court» or «Supreme Court of Colombia» in a climate context). Not naming the case does not exclude the taking up: what counts is the argumentative path and the formulas.

4. Almost the whole world reads Future Generations through the Dejusticia excerpts (11 pp.), not through the 51 Spanish pages. The English formulas of section 3 are those that non-Spanish-speaking courts echo, with the page numbers of the Spanish that Dejusticia reports in parentheses («p. 13», «p. 34», «p.45»).

## 1. The rulings

### 1.1 Tribunal Superior de Bogotá, Sala Civil Especializada en Restitución de Tierras, 12 February 2018 (first instance)

`decision_15f3`, 21 pp., Spanish, reporting judge Jorge Eliécer Moya Vargas, case number 110012203 000 2018 00319 00. Background at pp. 2-10 (subject matter, facts, responses of the 27 defendant entities, amici curiae), Considerations at pp. 10-20 (numbered 1-5), Resuelve at p. 21.

Reasoning. The legal issue (1, p. 10) is whether the tutela satisfies subsidiarity and immediacy; if so, whether the applicants, «miembros de las generaciones futuras», have standing, and whether the omissions of the authorities violate the right to a healthy environment and, consequently, life, health, food, and water. The tutela in principle does not protect collective rights, reserved for the acción popular (Art. 88 C.P.), but the Constitutional Court admits the exception under four conditions, from SU-1116/2001 (2, pp. 11-12): conexidad, directly injured applicant, non-hypothetical injury, order aimed at the fundamental right; plus the concrete inadequacy of the acción popular. This is followed by a review of the right to a healthy environment from Stockholm 1972 to the 1991 Constitution (3, pp. 12-14) and of climate change from UNFCCC and Kyoto to Paris, with the duty of participation (4, pp. 14-15). In the specific case (5, pp. 15-20) the Court recognizes «el papel protagónico de la Amazonía Colombiana respecto del clima global» (p. 15), the Amazon-Andes hydrological connection (p. 16), the government's commitments (INDC, Plan Nacional de Desarrollo, declaration with Germany, Norway, and the United Kingdom on zero net deforestation by 2020, p. 16) and the evidentiary weight of the AT-D bulletin and the EICDGB (p. 17); it does not doubt «de la legitimación activa de los accionantes en el presente caso» (p. 16). But it denies the remedy: «no es este excepcional remedio constitucional el mecanismo procedente para impartir las órdenes que aquí son objeto de petición», because the acción popular «refulge idóneo» both for the collective right and for the fundamental ones (p. 17); the judge of the acción popular retains enforcement jurisdiction and may establish a verification committee (p. 18); urgency does not remove adequacy, given the precautionary powers of Art. 17 of Ley 472 (p. 19); the perjuicio irremediable, the colonization of lands left by the FARC, «no obra acreditada siquiera en forma sumaria» (p. 20). In footnote 55 (p. 20) it distinguishes T-622/2016, where the acción popular had already been attempted without success. Resuelve: «NO CONCEDER el amparo solicitado» (p. 21).

Basis. Procedural: Arts. 86 and 88 C.P., Ley 472 de 1998, SU-1116/2001, T-061/2017, case law of the Supreme Court (STC10843-2015, SP13863-2014). It is the judgment that writes the polarities verbatim of `(principio della serie precedente)` and of `(principio della serie precedente)`: same lexicon (deforestación, urgencia, inminencia, gravedad, ambiente sano, generaciones futuras), reversed sign.

### 1.2 Dissenting opinion of Justice Óscar Humberto Ramírez Cardona, 12 February 2018

`opinion_e35a`, 18 pp., Spanish. In the corpus it bears `tipo_atto: decisione`: it is a dissent. Numbered 1.1-1.6 (role of the judge in the social state, pp. 1-4), 2.1-2.3 (procedural admissibility, pp. 4-12), 3.1-3.4 (the issues the majority should have addressed, pp. 12-18).

Reasoning. The majority did not examine the arguments on protection (2.3, p. 5): it acknowledges «derechos de primer orden» but gives precedence to the collective interest without a logical step (2.3.2.a, p. 6); the powers of the judge in the acción popular are not superior to those of the tutela judge (b, pp. 8-9); precautionary measures are not enough, because the applicants are «personas reales, que son las que acuden al amparo», the generation that will live through the 2041-2070 and 2071-2100 scenarios (c, p. 9); the harm is not hypothetical, deforestation grows by 44% and precaution covers uncertainty (d, pp. 10-12); the applicants also claim the rights «que asisten a la naturaleza en su calidad de sujeto de derecho» (p. 11). Four issues of constitutional relevance (3): «las generaciones futuras como sujeto de derechos» (3.1, p. 12, with T-411/92, T-445/16, C-389/16 and OC-23/17 at p. 14); «la Amazonia como sujeto de derechos» after the «giro ecocéntrico» (3.2, pp. 14-16, with T-622/16, Zaffaroni, Boff, Chico Mendes); precaution (3.3, pp. 16-17); the fundamental right to a written plan with participation (3.4, p. 17), with Laudato si' (written «Laudatio sí», pp. 17-18).

Basis. It anticipates the thesis later adopted by the Supreme Court, but along partly different paths: future generations as «sujeto de derechos» it takes from the sustainable development of the Constitutional Court (T-445/16: the rights of posterity «constituyen juridicamente un criterio limite al ejercicio de derechos actuales», p. 13), nature as a subject from the Atrato and Andean neoconstitutionalism, conexidad from OC-23/17. Between 12 February and 5 April 2018, a text can only draw on this dissent, not the Supreme Court. It is not a source of canonical.

### 1.3 Corte Suprema de Justicia, Sala de Casación Civil, STC4360-2018, 5 April 2018

`decision_7519`, 51 pp., full Spanish, rapporteur judge Luis Armando Tolosa Villabona, case number 11001-22-03-000-2018-00319-01, «Aprobado en sesión de cuatro de abril de dos mil dieciocho» (p. 1). Procedural history at pp. 1-10 (numbered 1-3, then 1.1 responses, 1.2 judgment under appeal, 1.3 appeal), Considerations at pp. 10-47 (numbered 1-14, with sub-numbers 5.1-5.3, 6.1-6.5, 11.1-11.3, 13.1-13.4), Decision at pp. 47-50. Signatures at pp. 50-51; Dejusticia (p. 11) lists three judges «issuing minority opinion» (García Restrepo, Rico Puerta, Salazar Ramírez): their dissenting opinions are not in the corpus.

Path. (a) Admissibility (1-3, pp. 10-15). The tutela action does not as a rule protect collective rights, but constitutional case law admits it under four conditions (1, pp. 10-11): «La conexidad entre la vulneración de derechos colectivos y la violación a uno u otros de tipo primario, fundamental e individual», the applicant directly harmed, the harm «plenamente probado» or «virtualmente amenazado», the order directed at individual rights. The protection of the environment «adquiere por “conexidad” la calidad de fundamental», and the tutela action prevails over the acción popular because the measures are urgent and «en la práctica resulta problemático delimitar el ámbito de aplicación de los dos instrumentos, ponderación en la cual deben primar los derechos fundamentales» (2, pp. 11-12); cited T-362/2014 and the Chamber's precedents (STC 7630/2016, 9813/2016, 15985/2017) on the tutela action for a healthy environment when it harms life, health, water (pp. 12-13). Hence the most quoted passage: «los derechos fundamentales de la vida, salud, el mínimo vital, la libertad y la dignidad humana están ligados sustancialmente y determinados por el entorno y el ecosistema. Sin ambiente sano los sujetos de derecho y los seres sintientes en general no podremos sobrevivir» (p. 13); the growing deterioration «es atentado grave para la vida actual y venidera» (p. 13); conclusion: «está suficientemente demostrada la procedencia excepcional de la tutela para resolver de fondo la problemática planteada» (p. 14). Minors have standing in their own right, because «la mayoría de edad no constituye un factor restrictivo frente a su ejercicio» (3, p. 14): the twelve named minors «acuden en defensa de sus prerrogativas e intereses y no en representación de un tercero» (p. 15).

(b) Basis (4-5, pp. 15-21). The diagnosis: growing threat «a la posibilidad de existencia del ser humano» (4, p. 15); humanity has adopted «un modelo antropocéntrico y egoísta» with three features, population growth, consumerism, exploitation (p. 16). A society «ecocéntrica antrópica» is born that overcomes the anthropocentric «homomensura» (5, pp. 16-17, with C-389/2016 on sustainable development). Every act that harms nature violates fundamental personal rights (5.1, p. 17); from Peces-Barba the passage from private ethics to public ethics and rights redefined as «derechos-deberes» (p. 18). The scope of protection includes the other, other species and «los sujetos aun no nacidos, quienes merecen disfrutar de las mismas condiciones medioambientales vividas por nosotros» (5.2, pp. 18-19), with Rodríguez Palop on diacronic solidarity (p. 19). The environmental rights of future generations are based on «deber ético de la solidaridad de la especie» and on «valor intrínseco de la naturaleza» (5.3, p. 19): natural assets also belong to descendants, «tributarios, destinatarios y titulares de ellos» (p. 20); the second basis «trasciende de la perspectiva antropocéntrica» and focuses on the criterion «ecocéntrico - antrópico», which «sitúa al ser humano a la par del entorno ecosistémico» (pp. 20-21); it follows that «una relación jurídica obligatoria de los derechos ambientales de las generaciones futuras, como la prestación de “no-hacer”, cuyo efecto se traduce en una limitación de la libertad de acción de las generaciones presentes» (p. 21).

(c) The sources (6-10, pp. 22-32). A «orden público ecológico mundial» of hard and soft law (6, p. 22): ICESCR art. 12, ENMOD and Protocol I, Stockholm 1972 quoted in full (pp. 23-24), Rio 1992 (pp. 24-25), Paris 2015 (p. 25, called «Convención Marco sobre el Cambio Climático de París de 2015»). The «orden público ecológico nacional» of the 1991 Constitution (7, p. 26) and the line of the Constitutional Court, the «Constitución Ecológica» (p. 27): T-411/1992 and C-431/2000 quoted in full (pp. 27-29), arts. 79 and 80 of the Constitution (8, p. 29), C-449/2015 on the triple dimension (p. 30). Conclusion reiterated: the tutela action proceeds «debido a la conexidad del medio ambiente sano con prerrogativas supralegales como la vida, la salud o la dignidad humana», the situation «afecta directamente derechos fundamentales individuales en cabeza no solo de los gestores, sino de todos los colombianos», which «permite concluir la ineficacia de la acción popular» (9, p. 30). «La conservación de la Amazonía es una obligación nacional y global», the «pulmón del mundo» (10, p. 30), with the Amazon Cooperation Treaty and the Paris commitment, Visión Amazonía (pp. 31-32).

(d) Facts, causation and principles (11-12, pp. 32-39). The question: «si en realidad existe un nexo causal entre el cambio climático generado por la reducción progresiva de la cobertura forestal» and damage to health, and whether the degradation of forests directly harms the applicants' dignified life, water and food (11, p. 33). The answer: from the EICDGB and IDEAM, «entre los años 2015 y 2016, la deforestación en la región Amazónica se incrementó en un 44%» (p. 34); the causes (land grabbing 60-65%, illicit crops 20-22%, mining 7-8%, infrastructure, agribusiness, timber); these factors cause «un perjuicio inminente y grave para los niños, adolescentes y adultos que acuden a esta acción, y en general, a todos los habitantes del territorio nacional, tanto para las generaciones presentes como las futuras», because deforestation «desboca incontroladamente la emisión de dióxido de carbono» and produces the greenhouse effect (pp. 34-35). The reality is measured by three principles: precaution, intergenerational equity, solidarity (p. 35). Precaution (11.1, pp. 35-37): «no cabe duda que existe peligro de daño», with the IDEAM scenarios of temperature and precipitation; irreversibility and scientific certainty «resultan evidentes» because the forestry sector accounts for 36% of emissions. Intergenerational equity (11.2, p. 37): «es obvia su transgresión», because the projections for 2041 and 2071 will affect «las futuras generaciones, entre ellos, los infantes que interponen esta salvaguarda, las que serán directamente afectadas, a menos que las presentes, reduzcan a cero la tasa de deforestación». Solidarity (11.3, p. 37): «deber y corresponsabilidad del Estado colombiano en detener las causas que provocan la emisión de GEI», with immediate mitigation measures to protect the applicants and all inhabitants of the planet, «incluido, los ecosistemas y seres vivos». A second paragraph, also numbered 11.3 (p. 38), adds the breach: «un grave desconocimiento de las obligaciones adquiridas por el Estado en la Convención Marco sobre el Cambio Climático de París de 2015», the commitment to zero deforestation by 2020 which would have avoided 44 megatonnes; eight issues to which the authorities must respond (pp. 38-39). Conclusion (12, p. 39): «la ineficacia de las medidas gubernamentales adoptadas para hacer frente al mismo» and the granting of the remedy for «palmario quebranto de garantías» fundamental «en conexidad con el medio ambiente»; the Chamber follows «la tesis sostenida por la Corte Constitucional en la sentencia T-622 de 2016, relacionada con el reconocimiento de la naturaleza como un auténtico sujeto de derechos», quoted in full (pp. 39-41).

(e) Omissions and remedy (13-14, pp. 41-47). «el Estado colombiano no ha enfrentado eficientemente la problemática de la deforestación en la Amazonía» (13, p. 41): the three CARs «no estan cumpliendo sus funciones de evaluar, controlar y monitorear» (13.1, p. 42); deforestation in the parks «es prueba de la omisión en el cumplimiento de las funciones legales» of PNN (13.2, p. 43); departments (13.3) and municipalities without updated POTs (13.4, pp. 43-45). Therefore: «en aras de proteger ese ecosistema vital para el devenir global, tal como la Corte Constitucional declaró al río Atrato, se reconoce a la Amazonía Colombiana como entidad, “sujeto de derechos”, titular de la protección, de la conservación, mantenimiento y restauración a cargo del Estado y las entidades territoriales que la integran» (14, p. 45). Orders (pp. 45-47, repeated in the operative part pp. 48-50): to the Presidency and ministries, within four months, «un plan de acción de corto, mediano y largo plazo, que contrarreste la tasa de deforestación en la Amazonia», with the participation of the applicants; within five months, «con la participación activa de los tutelantes», the «pacto intergeneracional por la vida del amazonas colombiano -PIVAC» with measures for «reducir a cero la deforestación y las emisiones de gases efecto invernadero»; to the municipalities, to update the POTs with a zero-deforestation plan; to the CARs, a plan with police, judicial and administrative measures; to all, «en las cuarenta y ocho (48) horas», to increase mitigation actions. Resuelve: REVOCAR the judgment under appeal; the file goes to the Corte Constitucional «para su eventual revisión» (p. 50).

Basis. Constitutional: Arts. 79, 80, 86, 88 C.P.; case law of the Corte Constitucional (T-411/1992, C-431/2000, C-449/2015, C-389/2016, T-362/2014, T-622/2016) and of the Chamber (STC 7630/2016); international law as «orden público ecológico mundial» (Stockholm, Rio, Paris, ICESCR, IACHR) and Paris as a breached commitment (p. 38); IDEAM and Ministry science as evidence of causation (pp. 33-37); doctrine (Peces-Barba, Rodríguez Palop, Jaquenod de Zsögön, Kormody, Roszak, Ruiz de la Peña, Mumford). No foreign cases: the Court does not cite Urgenda, Juliana, Leghari, OC-23/17, the Ecuadorian Constitution. No quota, no numerical reduction target set by the judge: the target (zero deforestation) is the one the State had set for itself in Paris. No article of the ECHR or the American Convention.

### 1.5 Party submissions and amicus

Tutela action (`complaint_7da3`, 122 pp., 29 January 2018, Dejusticia on behalf of 25 applicants aged between 7 and 25). The applicants declare themselves to be «la generación futura que enfrentará los efectos del cambio climático» (4.3.2, p. 63 and passim), base the admissibility of the action on SU-1116/2001, the rights of future generations on sustainable development and on T-622/2016 (pp. 38, 47-48, 68-70, 76), and invoke precaution, in dubio pro natura, intergenerational equity, solidarity (5.1, pp. 77 et seq.). It does not cite Urgenda, Juliana, Leghari, OC-23/17 (verified by text search). Appeal (`appeal_3c21`, 40 pp., 16 February 2018): insists on the suitability of the tutela action and on the irreparable harm proven, and adds OC-23/17 (pp. 7 and 34-35) as a source of the environment-human rights nexus. Amicus brief by James E. Hansen (`other_e010`, 4 pp., English, 16 March 2018, for attorney Daniel Galpern): the applicants are «twenty-five young persons, representing future generations» (p. 2); «the principle of intergenerational equity compels action without further delay so as not to burden disproportionately young persons and future generations» (p. 3); Colombia's reforestation potential «rivals that of the United States and the Russian Federation» (p. 4). The Court reports it in translation at p. 9 without adopting it as its own. Four interventions supported the application at first instance (Ministerio Público, Foro Nacional Ambiental, Julio Carrizosa, Universidad de los Andes); the Ministerio Público requested «se reconozca a la Amazonía como un sujeto de derechos de protección, conservación, mantenimiento y restauración» (Tribunal, 3.1, p. 7): the operative part of the Supreme Court's order originates from a request by a public party.

### 2.1 How to recognise the document from the reference

- Numbers 1-14 with sub-numbers (5.1-5.3, 6.1-6.5, 11.1-11.3, 13.1-13.4), or «párr./para./¶ 11.2»: Consideraciones of the Corte Suprema. Traps. «future generations» / «generaciones futuras» alone are not a marker of anything. «El principio de la equidad intergeneracional obliga a actuar sin más demora» (p. 9) and «the principle of intergenerational equity compels action without further delay» are by HANSEN, reported: whoever echoes them takes up the amicus, which circulates throughout the youth litigation. «sujeto de derechos», «entidad viviente», «sujetos de derechos individualizables», «la justicia con la naturaleza» are words from T-622. «tetula» is a typo from Youth Verdict repeated by an observation to OC-32/25. «Civil Appeals Court» is by Dejusticia.

## 4. The world around: competing sources

For each: what it states, on what basis, how it is recognised, whether it cites Future Generations. Entries 4.6-4.19 update and build on those in the Urgenda file.

### 4.1 Corte Constitucional, T-622/2016 (río Atrato), 10 November 2016

In the corpus: `atrato-river-decision-t-622-16-of-november-10-2016_976c`, Spanish (`judgment_dbe2`, 362 pp.) and English (`judgment_8646`). Protection of the ethnic communities of Chocó against illegal mining. The basis is the ecocentric approach and the «derechos bioculturales» (§ 5.11 et seq.); the key passage: «la justicia con la naturaleza debe ser aplicada más allá del escenario humano y debe permitir que la naturaleza pueda ser sujeto de derechos» (§ 9.31, p. 148); the Chamber makes «un paso adelante en la jurisprudencia» and declares the river a subject of rights, with guardians (representative of the State and of the communities), guardian commission, decontamination plans. No climate, no deforestation as a source of emissions, no future generation as rights-holder. It is recognized by: río Atrato, Chocó, minería ilegal, comunidades étnicas, derechos bioculturales, guardianes, «entidad viviente», «sujetos de derechos individualizables». It precedes Future Generations and cannot cite it. Rule: nature as a subject of rights in the abstract is T-622; the extension to an entire biome, with «devenir global», as a remedy for a climate injury to fundamental rights, is Future Generations. A text citing the two together (OC-32/25 note 532; Tribunal Administrativo de Santander 2025; observations to OC-32/25) makes a mediated reuse: stating in the basis which of the two carries the point.

### 4.2 The other judgments of the Corte Constitucional

Noted only through the Corte Suprema, the Tribunale, the salvamento and the tutela [texts not in the corpus]. T-411/1992: the natural heritage «pertenece a las personas que en él viven, pero también a las generaciones venideras» and «El hombre no es el amo omnipotente del universo» (STC4360, p. 28): it is the closest origin and the most cited in Colombia for future generations as recipients of a legacy. C-431/2000: the environment «indispensable para su supervivencia y la de las generaciones futuras» and the «Constitución ecológica» (p. 28). C-449/2015: the healthy environment as a principle, fundamental and collective right, obligation, the «triple dimensión» (p. 30). C-389/2016: sustainable development, «las actuaciones presentes deben asegurar la posibilidad de aprovechamiento de los recursos en el porvenir» (p. 17). T-362/2014 and SU-1116/2001: the tutela proceeds by conexidad when the collective harms the fundamental one (pp. 12-13; Tribunale pp. 11-12). T-445/2016: the rights of posterity as «criterio limite al ejercicio de derechos actuales» (salvamento, p. 13). Rule: whoever sets out the conexidad, the heritage to be delivered, the Constitución ecológica, the triple dimensión, without climate and without the specific moves (seres sintientes, no-hacer, ecocéntrico-antrópico, bioma subject), cites the Corte Constitucional: ALTRA_FONTE. Whoever applies those ideas to deforestation as a climatic cause and to young claimants as an injured future generation draws on Future Generations.

### 4.3 The Colombian Constitution

Arts. 79 and 80 quoted in full (STC4360, p. 29), art. 86 (tutela) and art. 88 (acción popular). «Todas las personas tienen derecho a gozar de un ambiente sano» and «Es deber del Estado proteger la diversidad e integridad del ambiente» are the Constitution, not the judgment. Indicators: whoever writes «art. 79» or «derecho a gozar de un ambiente sano» without climate conexidad is referring to the Constitution.

### 4.4 Stockholm, Rio, Paris, UNFCCC

Stockholm 1972: proclamations 6-9 quoted in full (pp. 23-24); «meta imperiosa de la humanidad» and «para las generaciones presentes y futuras» are from 1972. Rio 1992: only the conference and its instruments (pp. 24-25); Principle 3 is not in the file [not verified in the corpus]. Paris: the Court calls it «Convención Marco sobre el Cambio Climático de París de 2015» and cites its cooperation (p. 25), the Colombian commitment to reduce deforestation (p. 32) and its non-compliance (p. 38). UNFCCC: named by the Tribunal (4, p. 14) with the definition of climate change, not by the Supreme Court. Rule: deforestation as a source of emissions taken from the UNFCCC (sinks, art. 4(1)(d)) or the non-compliance with an NDC taken from Paris is ANOTHER_SOURCE; deforestation as «perjuicio inminente y grave» to the fundamental rights of present and future generations, with the State's co-responsibility based on solidarity, is Future Generations. A court that says that the Colombian courts have imposed «acordes con las NDCs» measures (observation to OC-32/25, `other_90c1`, p. 75) reads Future Generations from the Paris side: it is axis 11.3 (p. 38), not promoted to a probe.

### 4.5 Inter-American Court, OC-23/17 (15/11/2017)

`...-opinion_029c` (Spanish), requested by Colombia. Healthy environment as an autonomous right and interdependence with life and integrity; duties of prevention and precaution; jurisdiction for effective control. It precedes Future Generations and does not cite it. The Supreme Court does NOT cite it; the impugnación (pp. 7 and 34-35) and the salvamento (p. 14: «la Corte Interamericana de Derechos Humanos destacó la interdependencia entre el derecho al ambiente sano y los derechos humanos») cite it. Rule: the environment-human rights nexus in the Inter-American lexicon («interdependencia», «derecho autónomo», arts. 4 and 5 ACHR, Protocol of San Salvador) is OC-23/17; in the region it is the most likely source of an unattributed borrowing. The Colombian nexus has a different lexicon: «conexidad», «tutela», «acción popular», «iusfundamental», «supralegal».

### 4.6 Urgenda (Rechtbank 2015, Hof 2018, Hoge Raad 2019)

`urgenda-foundation-v-state-of-the-netherlands-decision_f1ad`, `..._decision_8a5e`, `..._judgment_ed17`. Obligation of the State to reduce emissions by at least 25% by 2020, arising from the duty of care (2015) and from Articles 2 and 8 ECHR (2018-2019); its own share; minimum derived from scientific consensus; order as to result. Future generations appear therein as beneficiaries of sustainability and equity (Rechtbank 4.8, 4.57, 4.76, 4.88-4.89), never as rights-holders: the Hof decides that the current generation suffices, «in particular but not limited to the younger individuals», and that there is no need to rule on future generations (§ 37); the Hoge Raad refers to Article 3(1) UNFCCC on present and future generations (5.7.3). The emissions are from fossil fuels, not from deforestation; the remedy is a percentage target, not the legal personality of a biome nor a participatory plan. It differs from Future Generations in: ECHR, «duty of care», «its part», 25-40%, carbon budget, Annex I, IPCC AR4. It cites Future Generations: the three judgments do not (the Hoge Raad is from 2019 and does not name it). It is cited by Urgenda's defense statement in cassation (`urgenda-foundation-v-state-of-the-netherlands-appeal_2adc`, 12 April 2019, Dutch, 228 pp.): § 14, p. 13, in a list with Massachusetts v. EPA and Leghari, and § 455, p. 165, as a judgment «principiële» according to which «toekomstige generaties de houders van subjectieve rechten kunnen zijn», with the Dejusticia passages on p. 13 and 5.3. It is a party document: the Supreme Court did not follow it on this point.

### 4.7 Neubauer et al. v. Germany (BVerfG, 24/3/2021)

`neubauer-et-al-v-germany-order_7977` (English), `..._order_c68f` (German). The exact opposite on the point of future generations. § 109 (p. 33): «The complainants are not asserting the rights of unborn persons or even of entire future generations, neither of whom enjoy subjective fundamental rights»; § 146 (p. 42): the duty of protection under Art. 2(2) GG may extend to future generations, but «this duty to afford intergenerational protection has a solely objective dimension because future generations – either as a whole or as the sum of individuals not yet born – do not yet carry any fundamental rights in the present»; p. 61: «future generations - those who will be most affected - naturally have no voice of their own in shaping the current political agenda». The violation lies in the future freedom of the living (intertemporal guarantees of freedom, Art. 20a GG, remaining budget). The Amazon appears there only as an element of the tipping of the climate system (p. 20). It is recognized by: Art. 20a GG, «intertemporal», «objective», Climate Protection Act, budget. Cites Future Generations: no. Rule: future generations as HOLDERS of rights that limit the present = Future Generations (and T-411/T-445 in Colombia); future generations as the object of an objective duty, with the freedom of the living as the right = Neubauer. Many texts place them side by side (observations to OC-32/25, written statements to the ICJ, Do-hyun Kim): state which of the two carries the proposition.

### 4.8 Verein KlimaSeniorinnen Schweiz v. Switzerland (ECtHR, GC, 9/4/2024)

`verein-klimaseniorinnen-schweiz-and-others-v-switzerland-judgment_f4b1`. Art. 8 ECHR; «intergenerational burden-sharing» (§ 410, p. 168; § 420, p. 170: «both in regard to the different generations of those currently living and in regard to future generations»), but the obligations «extend to those individuals currently alive who, at a given time, fall within the jurisdiction» (§ 420). No future title-holders. It cites Future Generations: the judgment does not; Colombia appears only in a UN report cited (p. 75) and in the request for OC-32 (p. 107). It is cited by the application (`..._application_0c7a`, p. 20, note 75: «Supreme Court of Colombia, Future Generations v Ministry of Environment and Others, 4 April 2018, p. 10-11, §§ 11.2, 11.3») and by three third-party interventions (`..._other_8afe`, p. 9, on the remedies ordered; `..._other_7ae3`, p. 2; `..._other_8a52`, p. 6). Party submissions and interventions: the Court does not make the proposition its own.

### 4.11 Leghari v. Pakistan (Lahore High Court, 2015-2018)

`leghari-v-federation-of-pakistan-decision_0d83`, `..._judgment_5be2`. Life and dignity (arts. 9 and 14 of the Constitution) include the environment; delay in adaptation; Climate Change Commission. No European court has so far taken up Future Generations in its reasoning; the courts that do so are in Colombia, Australia (Queensland), the Netherlands (Shell 2024), Brazil (STF, in a list), Korea (NHRC), the Philippines (CHR) and the Inter-American Court (in a note).

## 5. How to recognise a resumption

### 5.1 Strong indications
- Name in compound form, reference number STC4360-2018 (also «STC-4360», «STC 4360/2018», «TC4360»), radicación 11001-22-03-000-2018-00319-01/-00, names of the parties (Lozano Barragán, Salamanca Mancera, Rodríguez Peña, «Barragán»), reporting judge Tolosa Villabona; «Colombian Supreme Court», «Supreme Court of Colombia», «Corte Suprema de Justicia de Colombia» in a climate or Amazonian context.
- A Spanish page number transmitted by Dejusticia («p. 13», «p. 34», «p. 45», «pp. 48-49») or a paragraph 11.1-11.3, 14: decipher it with 2.1.
- The formulas in section 3 or their faithful translations: fundamental rights «determinados por el entorno y el ecosistema» / «determined by the environment and the ecosystem»; «sujetos de derecho y los seres sintientes» / «subjects of law and sentient beings»; «relación jurídica obligatoria» and «no-hacer» / «binding legal relationship» and «omission», «limitación de la libertad de acción de las generaciones presentes»; «ecocéntrico - antrópico» / «ecocentric- anthropic», «a la par»; «perjuicio inminente y grave» / «imminent and serious damage» from deforestation; «es obvia su transgresión» / «the transgression is obvious» with the minor applicants «directamente afectadas» / «directly affected»; «deber y corresponsabilidad» / «duty and co-responsibility»; «entidad, “sujeto de derechos”, titular de la protección, de la conservación, mantenimiento y restauración» / «entitled to protection, conservation, maintenance and restoration»; «pacto intergeneracional» / «intergenerational pact», PIVAC.
- Two or more links in the Court's reasoning (conexidad and procedibilidad, rights determined by the ecosystem and future generations as rights-holders, deforestation as a climate source with the three principles, breach, subject biome and participatory plans), also with other sources alongside.
- The reasoning whereby the young applicants ARE the future generation and the harm is already current because the projections fall within their lifetime: this is the move of 11.2, which neither Rio nor Brundtland nor Hansen make.

### 5.2 False friends
T-622/2016 (río Atrato, derechos bioculturales, guardianes, comunita' etniche, «entidad viviente», «sujetos de derechos individualizables», «justicia con la naturaleza»); the Constitutional Court on conexidad in the abstract (SU-1116, T-362: «la acción de tutela es procedente dada la imposibilidad en la mayoría de los casos de separar los ámbitos de protección de los dos grupos de derechos»), on the heritage of future generations (T-411), on the Constitución ecológica (C-431), on the triple dimensión (C-449), on desarrollo sostenible (C-389); art. 79 C.P.; Stockholm («generaciones presentes y futuras», «meta imperiosa»), Rio 3, the UNFCCC preamble, Brundtland; Paris and the NDC read per se; OC-23/17 (interdependence, autonomous right, effective control); Neubauer (intertemporal freedom, objective duty, art. 20a GG); Urgenda (ECHR, duty of care, quota, 25%); KlimaSeniorinnen (burden-sharing among the living); Juliana (public trust, danger creation); Leghari (life includes the environment, adaptation); Ecuador and Te Awa Tupua (subjectivity by constitution or law); Hansen (intergenerational equity as an appeal to act, cited at p. 9); Peces-Barba and Rodríguez Palop in the form of the source. Polarity: those who assert the inadmissibility of protection for collective rights (Putumayo, p. 7; Tribunal Superior, p. 17), future harm not proven or speculative (Tribunal, p. 20; Duarte; Ninth Circuit), future generations not rights-holders (Neubauer § 146), nature as an object, do not take up the proposition; those who reject those theses with the words of the Court do so.

### 5.3 Uncertain cases
- Future generations «titolari» without referral, in a Latin American text after 5/4/2018: it may come from T-411/1992 (assets to be handed over), from sustainable development, from OC-32/25 (of 29/5/2025). Signals of Future Generations: «relación jurídica obligatoria», «no-hacer», «limitación de la libertad de acción de las generaciones presentes», «solidaridad de la especie» with «valor intrínseco», the applicants as a future generation, the intergenerational pact. Signals from another source: «legado», «entregar ... a nuestros descendientes», «Constitución ecológica», «desarrollo sostenible» as a policy principle. If nothing is decided and the text follows the rings in the Court's order, in doubt the resumption prevails.
- Nature as a subject of rights without referral: if the subject is a river, a páramo, a forest with guardians and communities, it is the Atrato or the line descending from it (STL10716-2020 on Nevados, Santurbán in 2025); if it is a biome «vital para el devenir global», recognized as a remedy for climate harm, it is Future Generations. If it cites both together, mediated resumption: state which one carries the point.
- Deforestation as a climate source: in a Brazilian text it may come from Brazilian law (PSB, Código Florestal, Fundo Amazônia). Signals of Future Generations: «perjuicio inminente y grave», present and future, State co-responsibility for solidarity, the three principles in the order precaution-equity-solidarity.
- Conexidad and tutela: in a non-climate Colombian text it is SU-1116/T-362 (ALTRA_FONTE); in a climate or Amazonian text declaring the ineffectiveness of the acción popular due to urgency and the impossibility of separating the two instruments, it is Future Generations.

### 5.4 Mediated reproductions
OC-32/25 (notes 378, 396, 532, 560), Shell 2024 (p. 21 and note 23), Youth Verdict [1472], the Tribunal Administrativo de Santander 2025 (pp. 42-44), the Korean NHRC (p. 11) build their point, in whole or in part, on Future Generations. If the passage reproduces the proposition from one of these points, the chain passes through Future Generations: indicate the intermediate source in «fondamento» and flag the reproduction as nested (codebook, dimension «annidata») instead of classifying it as ALTRA_FONTE. If it reproduces it from a point that does not rest on Future Generations (OC-32/25 on the healthy climate or on debida diligencia; Santurbán on the páramos from T-622), it is ALTRA_FONTE. The Dejusticia excerpts are a medium, not a source: whoever cites «Dejusticia» or «unofficial translation» cites the judgment.

### 5.5 Who is speaking, and the group's rules on what counts as a reuse

Rules decided by the PI on 23/9/2026, valid for all cases.

1. The same rationale applied to a PRIVATE COMPANY also counts. If a text states that deforestation harms the fundamental rights of present and future generations, or that the Amazon is a subject of rights, in order to ground a duty of a company (Shell 2024, p. 21; AIDESEP v. Louis Dreyfus, p. 48; DAMSHEG v. MACH Energy, ¶ 44), it is a reuse: the addressee changes, the proposition does not. State this in «mossa».
2. It also counts if it is stated by a PARTY, an INTERVENER, or an AMICUS. The verweerschrift of Urgenda (p. 165), the application in KlimaSeniorinnen (note 75), the amici in Juliana and Lighthiser, the written statements to the ICJ, the observations on OC-32/25 all reuse Future Generations even if the court does not follow them: classify as REUSE and indicate who is speaking.
3. It also counts if the COURT summarizes it as a party's argument, and in that case it must be flagged. The Prague Tribunal (p. 5) lists Future Generations among the precedents invoked by the applicants; the Colombian Supreme Court itself reports the applicants' self-characterization (2.5, p. 4) and Hansen (p. 9). In these cases the reuse exists, but in «mossa» it must be stated that the adjudicating body is reporting, not adopting; and if it then adopts it (11.2 makes the self-characterization its own), the two moments must be distinguished.

The name may appear in a comparative list (Brazilian STF, Klimaatzaak, Notre Affaire, Greenpeace SE Asia) without any proposition being reused: in that case it is NON_AFFERMA with mention, not REUSE. What counts is the content.
