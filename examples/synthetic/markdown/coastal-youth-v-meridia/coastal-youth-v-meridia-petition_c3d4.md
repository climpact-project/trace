PETITION OF THE COASTAL YOUTH ALLIANCE
This document is invented. It exists only to try the TRACE code.
The petitioners are young people who live on the coast of Meridia.
Courts in several jurisdictions have accepted claims of this kind (see Example Foundation v. State of Exempla;
Northfield Residents Association v. Republic of Caldera; Hill Farmers v. Commonwealth of Ostra).
The petitioners set out the facts below.
1

<!-- page 2 -->

The facts are not disputed by the Government.
The Government knows that the coast is eroding faster every year and that the young people living there are exposed.
Such knowledge gives rise to a duty to act with reasonable care and without delay against the risk to the
petitioners.1
The Government has adopted no measure since its last plan expired.
The petitioners ask the Court to find that this silence breaches the Constitution.
2
1 Example Foundation v. State of Exempla (Supreme Court of Exempla, 2019), para. 4.2.

<!-- page 3 -->

REQUESTS
A duty of care that is never acted upon offers no protection to those who are exposed to the risk.
The petitioners therefore ask the Court to order the Government to adopt a coastal protection plan within one year.
They also ask the Court to keep the case open until the plan is adopted.
