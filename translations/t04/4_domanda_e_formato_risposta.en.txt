QUESTION. For EVERY principle of the list (none excluded): does the citation take it? To take = to use, apply, discuss, reject or report the reasoning of the case on that point (general rules G01-G08). For each one reason: which sentence the citation supports, what it attributes to the case, whether it is that principle in the light of definition, distinctive element, exclusions and boundaries.
But first: does the passage really CITE or TAKE UP the case (not an index, not a namesake, not another source)?
